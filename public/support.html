Help and Support

Galactic Outbreak, a public health strategy game by Adam Troy.

How do I play?

Each day you get a few actions. Pick a tool from the tray, then tap people or links in the network. Vaccinate to protect, isolate to stop spread, and sever links to cut the busiest routes. Keep total cases under the cap until the outbreak dies out.

Is the science real?

The game is simplified, but the ideas are real epidemiology: super-spreaders and hubs, herd immunity, the effective reproduction number R, and why isolation early beats treatment late. Each level names the idea it teaches.

Purchases

  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that turns off the ads between levels.
  • On a new device, open Settings and tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device only. It is lost if the app is deleted or Reset progress is used. Cloud saves are not available yet.
