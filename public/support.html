Help and Support

Galactic Outbreak, a public health strategy game by Adam Troy.

How do I play?

Each day you get a few actions. Tap a red line to cut it, or a person to protect or isolate them. The tray at the bottom shows your tools: Sever cuts the busiest routes, Vaccinate protects a healthy person, Quarantine stops a sick person from passing it on. Some tools open on later days, and the briefing says when. Later planets add two more: Test finds people who carry it before they feel sick, and Mask halves the spread on every line of the person wearing it. Keep total cases under the cap until the outbreak dies out. Sick people who go untreated can die, so act early.

It is too hard, or I am stuck

Tap Hint in a level for a suggested move, and Undo to take back your last one. For a calmer game, open Settings on the star map and turn on Gentle mode: the spread is slower and you get one extra use of each tool every day. Stars still count.

Is the science real?

The game is simplified, but the ideas are real epidemiology: super-spreaders and hubs, herd immunity, the effective reproduction number R, why isolation early beats treatment late, and how deadly each disease really is. Each level names the idea it teaches. The game is a teaching model, not medical advice.

Purchases

  • Purchases are part of the iPhone app from the App Store. The web version has none.
  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that turns off the ads between levels.
  • On a new device, open Settings (the gear on the star map) and tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device only. It is lost if the app is deleted, if the browser's site data is cleared, or if Reset progress is used. Cloud saves are not available yet. A Remove Ads purchase is never lost: Restore purchases brings it back.
