Privacy Policy

VAXrebot, a public health strategy game by Adam Troy. Last updated October 17, 2026.

The short version

VAXrebot does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or photos. There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your device.

What the game stores

  • Your level progress, stars, in-game funding, and power-up charges.
  • Whether you have seen the how-to-play screen, whether music is on, and whether you own Remove Ads.

This is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app erases it. Settings, Reset progress erases your level progress.

Ads in the free version

The free version of VAXrebot will show short ads between levels, never during a level. Ads will be served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect device information such as your IP address, device type, and ad interactions, as described in Google's advertising policies.

VAXrebot does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, and it never shows Apple's tracking prompt. Every ad it requests is non-personalized, so ads are not based on your interests or history. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Buying Remove Ads turns ads off completely.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. We may receive a record that a purchase was made so the game can unlock it and restore it on a new device.

Children

VAXrebot is a general audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? See Help and support.
