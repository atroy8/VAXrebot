# Categories

App Store Connect, your app, General, App Information, Category.

| Field | Pick | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary category | Games | It is a game first. |
| Games subcategory 1 | Puzzle | Each level is a small turn-based puzzle with one best line of play. This is where players who like it will look. |
| Games subcategory 2 | Strategy | Limited moves, planning ahead, power-ups and upgrades. |
| Secondary category | Education | The game teaches real epidemiology, and Education is where teachers and parents browse. |

Do not tick "Made for Kids" and do not choose the Kids category. The free version shows third-party ads, and the Kids category does not allow that kind of advertising. The age rating (see `age-rating.md`) already tells parents who it suits.

Alternative Adam may prefer: Games subcategory 2 = Educational instead of Strategy, with the secondary category left empty. Puzzle should stay first either way.
