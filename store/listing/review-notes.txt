Thank you for reviewing Galactic Outbreak.

NO LOGIN. There are no accounts and no sign in. The game starts straight away and works offline. Progress is saved on the device only.

HOW TO PLAY IN THREE LINES
1. Tap Play, then Play level 1, then Begin Containment. Faces are people, lines are contacts, red faces are sick, red lines are where it will spread tonight (spread is certain: every red line crosses unless the player acts).
2. Tap a red line to cut it (Sever Link). From day 2 you can tap Vaccinate and then a healthy person, from day 3 Quarantine and then a sick person.
3. Tap Next day. You win when nobody is sick, and lose if cases reach the cap shown at the top.

AFTER A LEVEL. The results screen says how few cases the best play allowed. Show best play replays those moves step by step on the same web, and Retry this web deals the same puzzle again.

WHERE THINGS ARE
- Settings: tap Play on the title screen, then the gear button at the top right of the star map. The gear is hidden on the title screen and during a level.
- Remove Ads (non-consumable in-app purchase, product id remove_ads): Settings, first section, Remove Ads.
- Restore purchases: Settings, first section, Restore purchases.
- Privacy policy and Help and support: Settings, About section. Both open in Safari.
- Gentle mode (easier play): Settings, Play section.

ADS. Short interstitial ads can appear only between levels, when leaving the results screen. None appears before the player has finished two levels, then at most one per two finished levels. There are no ads during a level, no rewarded ads and no banners. Ads are requested as non-personalized and the app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

CONTENT. This is an educational puzzle about outbreak response. People in a level can die if the outbreak is not contained. This is shown as a grey token with a white flower and a number, with no graphic imagery. The diseases are invented and each is modelled on a real one, which the game names. The game is not medical advice and says so in its help page.

AUDIENCE. General audience. The app is not in the Kids category and is not directed to children under 13.
